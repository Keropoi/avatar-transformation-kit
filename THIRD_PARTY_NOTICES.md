# Third-party dependencies

Core calls Unity, VRChat SDK, Modular Avatar/NDMF, lilToon, and VRCFury APIs. Optional adapters integrate DressingTools/DressingFramework and the historical Poiyomi material setup. Install dependencies separately under their respective licenses; none of their source or binaries is bundled here.

Every user purchases/imports the external Angelring Halo and supplies their own A/B outfits. Halo meshes, textures, materials, animations, particles, audio, and local product derivatives are excluded from this distribution. Generated data stays in the consuming project.

The repository author plans to obtain the halo creator's authorization; no completed authorization record is currently supplied. Do not treat this preview as a creator endorsement or permission grant. Product permissions are separate from the code's UNLICENSED status.

The documented lilToon 2.3.2 workaround identifies three inactive include comments; no third-party shader file is redistributed. See Documentation~/BOOTH_ASSETS.md, ADAPTERS.md, and COMPATIBILITY.md for asset and dependency scope.
